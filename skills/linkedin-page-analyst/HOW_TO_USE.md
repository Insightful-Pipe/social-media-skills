# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "linkedin-page-analyst" skill. Analyse our LinkedIn company page for the last 30 days: are we gaining the right followers, and which posts send people to our site?

## What you need

- **Your LinkedIn company page.** Either connect it through the InsightfulPipe MCP (LinkedIn Pages), or export the page's Visitors, Followers and Content analytics files from LinkedIn and upload them.
- **Your target buyer:** the job functions, seniorities and company sizes you sell to. Without it, the skill uses Manager and above and doesn't grade fit by function.
- **Optional:** GA4 connected, to see the site visits and key events each post sends; LinkedIn Ads connected, to price paid followers; last month's follower snapshot from this skill, to see who your new followers are; and your own personal post analytics export, if you post as a founder.

## Example requests

**Monthly page report**
Hey Claude, I just added the "linkedin-page-analyst" skill. Give me our LinkedIn company page analytics for last month against the month before.

**Are we growing with the right people?**
Hey Claude, I just added the "linkedin-page-analyst" skill. We sell to operations and finance leaders at companies with 51 to 500 staff. Check whether our new LinkedIn followers match that, organic and paid apart.

**Why isn't the page growing?**
Hey Claude, I just added the "linkedin-page-analyst" skill. Our LinkedIn page has barely grown in three months. Audit the page and tell me how to grow LinkedIn company page followers from here.

**From exports**
Hey Claude, I just added the "linkedin-page-analyst" skill. Here are our LinkedIn Visitors, Followers and Content exports. Which posts, formats and days work best for us?

**Founder and page side by side**
Hey Claude, I just added the "linkedin-page-analyst" skill. I pasted my personal LinkedIn post analytics. Compare my posts with the company page's.

## What you get

1. **A scorecard for seven areas:** follower growth, follower fit, page visitors, content reach, posts and formats, site visits from LinkedIn, and page setup.
2. **The top actions,** each with the numbers behind it and what to check next month.
3. **Follower fit:** the share of your followers, and of your new followers, in the functions, seniorities and company sizes you sell to, organic and paid apart.
4. **Post rankings** against your page's own median clicks per 1,000 impressions, plus the best formats, topics and posting days from your own history. Groups with fewer than 3 posts are marked as too few to call.
5. **Site visits per post** from GA4, matched by utm_content or by landing page.
6. **A page setup check:** About, website, logo, cover, industry, size, headquarters, specialties and showcase pages.
7. **A follower snapshot** to keep, so next month's run can show who your new followers are.

## What it changes

Nothing. It reads only. It never posts, comments, reacts or edits your page or campaigns; when a fix needs a change, it writes the change out for you to make.

## Tips

- Run it monthly, and keep the snapshot it gives you.
- Tag the links in your posts with utm_content so GA4 can credit each post exactly.
- A post needs 300 impressions and 7 days before it is ranked, so very new pages may see few rankings at first.
