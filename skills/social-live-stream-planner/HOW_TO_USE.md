# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "social-live-stream-planner" skill. Plan our next YouTube Live: build the run of show from where viewers left our past streams, and draft the promotion posts.

## What you need

- **The stream:** platform, date, start time with your time zone, how long it runs, the topic, the host and any guests.
- **Your planned segments,** if you have them. Without them, the skill drafts a structure from the topic.
- **The offer,** if you'll make one: what it is, the discount or next step, the code, how long it runs, and the link.
- **Your past streams' numbers,** if you have them: views, watch time, average view duration, peak concurrent viewers, and roughly when you pitched. A YouTube Studio, TikTok LIVE Center, Instagram, LinkedIn or X export works, or just read the numbers out.
- **Optional: the InsightfulPipe MCP with your YouTube channel added.** The skill then finds your past streams itself, splits live and replay viewing, and reads each replay's retention curve to see where people left. Add Facebook Pages for Facebook Live replays, and the Social Media Scheduler to check promotion slots against your queue and save the posts as drafts once you say yes.

## Example requests

**Plan a YouTube Live from past streams**
Hey Claude, I just added the "social-live-stream-planner" skill. Our monthly YouTube Live is on the 12th at 7 p.m. New York time, 50 minutes, with a guest. Time the polls and our offer from where viewers dropped off in our last four streams.

**TikTok LIVE for a brand**
Hey Claude, I just added the "social-live-stream-planner" skill. We're doing our first TikTok LIVE to show a product launch. Here are our LIVE Center numbers from two test streams. Plan the run of show, the moderation and the promotion.

**LinkedIn Live or an X Space**
Hey Claude, I just added the "social-live-stream-planner" skill. Plan a 40-minute LinkedIn Live panel with two customers, plus an X Space the next day for follow-up questions. Tell me what each platform needs before the date.

**Promotion posts into the scheduler**
Hey Claude, I just added the "social-live-stream-planner" skill. Draft the announcement, reminder, starting-soon and replay posts for Instagram, Facebook and X, work around what's already queued, and save them as drafts. Show me every post first.

**After the stream**
Hey Claude, I just added the "social-live-stream-planner" skill. Our stream was two days ago. Compare it with our past streams, tell me if the prompts held people, and plan the replay chapters and clips.

## What you get

1. **Where viewers left your past streams:** the minutes they dropped, the windows that repeat across streams, and whether your pitch itself was losing people.
2. **The run of show:** segments with times and who's on, and every cue (prompt or offer mention) with the minute, the line to say and why it sits there.
3. **Changes to your plan,** each with its evidence, such as moving a pitch that lost viewers.
4. **Moderation and setup:** roles, the moderator's jobs, chat settings, and the platform's requirements to check before the date.
5. **A promotion schedule:** six slots per network, moved off days that already have a post, with character counts against each network's limit.
6. **Benchmarks to beat,** and after the stream, results against them, replay chapters and clip candidates.
7. **Draft posts,** ready to save, marked "not run" until you confirm.

## What it can change for you

With your yes on the exact list, it can save the promotion posts as drafts in the InsightfulPipe Social Media Scheduler for Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, X, TikTok and YouTube. It schedules them live only if you ask. It never starts or schedules the stream itself and never changes your chat settings.

## Tips

- Note the minute you pitch in each stream. It's how the skill tells whether the pitch is what loses people.
- Give it at least two past streams with replays. With one, it says the timing rests on a single stream.
- Paste peak concurrent viewers from YouTube Studio after each stream; the benchmarks get better every month.
- Run it again two days after the stream to see whether the new prompts held viewers.
