# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "video-marketing-strategy" skill. Plan our video program for next quarter: which networks, how often, and what to film with the hours we have.

## What you need

- **Your team's real weekly hours for video,** editing included. The whole cadence is built from this number.
- **Optional: the InsightfulPipe MCP connected to Claude,** with any of YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, a Facebook Page, a LinkedIn Page and GA4 added. The skill reads your last 180 days of posts itself.
- **No connection?** Describe the team and paste what you have: a YouTube Studio content table, Instagram or Facebook post insights, a LinkedIn analytics export. With no history at all, it still plans from your description and sets the baselines after the first cycle.
- **TikTok watch time,** pasted from TikTok Studio's post list. The TikTok API gives views but not watch time.

## Example requests

**Full plan**
Hey Claude, I just added the "video-marketing-strategy" skill. We have 16 hours a week for video and want more sign-ups. Build our video marketing strategy across YouTube, Instagram, TikTok and Facebook.

**YouTube and short-form together**
Hey Claude, I just added the "video-marketing-strategy" skill. Should we make long YouTube videos, or only Shorts, Reels and TikToks? Use our own numbers.

**Is video worth it here?**
Hey Claude, I just added the "video-marketing-strategy" skill. Check where our videos beat our photo and text posts, network by network, before we spend more time on video.

**A plan template from a description**
Hey Claude, I just added the "video-marketing-strategy" skill. We're a two-person team with no video yet. Give us a video marketing plan we can run in 8 hours a week.

## What you get

1. **Where video already wins:** for each network, your video posts against your static posts, on that network's own count, plus watch minutes per video.
2. **A role for each network** (Lead, Support, Test or Hold), with the score and the reason.
3. **A cadence from your hours,** with the arithmetic shown so you can change any figure.
4. **Pillars and named series,** each built on one of your past winners, with a target length or the second by which the main answer must land.
5. **A 4-week calendar** with shoot days, filming sessions and what goes out each week, and the chain from each long video to its shorts.
6. **KPIs per network,** defined the way each network counts a view, with your own baseline and a target from your own best quarter of videos.

## Good to know

- It never adds up views across networks, because YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook and LinkedIn each count a view differently. Cross-network totals use watch minutes, follows and site visits.
- It reads only. It never uploads, posts or schedules anything.
- The LinkedIn Page reads are untested until a LinkedIn Page is connected; a pasted LinkedIn export works today.

## Tips

- Time a few of your own pieces first. Real hours per piece make the cadence far more accurate than the defaults.
- Run it again after week 4 to check the KPIs and decide whether the Test network stays.
