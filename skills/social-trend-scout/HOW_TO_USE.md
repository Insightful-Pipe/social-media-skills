# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "social-trend-scout" skill. Find this week's social media trends in my niche and tell me which ones are worth posting about.

## What you need

- **Your niche in a few lines:** what you sell, who buys it, and 5 to 15 topics you can talk about with authority.
- **Topics to stay away from,** if you have any. Tragedies, crime, politics and health scares are always left out.
- **TikTok trends, pasted.** No connector reads TikTok trends, so copy the TikTok Creative Center Trends list for your market (hashtags and songs, with rank and rank change).
- **Optional: the InsightfulPipe MCP** connected to Claude, with X, Pinterest, YouTube, TikTok and DataForSEO. Each one replaces a paste: X trends and post counts, Pinterest Trends, YouTube uploads, Google search interest and your own past posts are then read live.
- **Optional: how many trends your team can make content for this week.** Default: 3.

## Example requests

**This week's trends**
Hey Claude, I just added the "social-trend-scout" skill. What's trending in my niche this week? I sell houseplants and plant-care supplies. Here is the TikTok Creative Center list.

**TikTok trends this week**
Hey Claude, I just added the "social-trend-scout" skill. Here are this week's TikTok trending hashtags and sounds. Which ones fit my skincare brand, and which have already peaked?

**Around a date**
Hey Claude, I just added the "social-trend-scout" skill. Black Friday is in four weeks. Find the rising topics around it for my coffee shop and give me a concept per network.

**No connection**
Hey Claude, I just added the "social-trend-scout" skill. I have no accounts connected. Here are my topics, a Google Trends CSV and my 15 best posts. Tell me what to make this week.

## What you get

1. **A short list of trends to act on this week,** capped to what your team can make, each with its slope, where the slope came from, and its fit, staying power and effort scores.
2. **A concept per network** for each of those trends: format, hook, beats, on-screen text or title, caption, call to action and a post-by date, each borrowing from one of your own past winning posts when you have one.
3. **Dated moments** (a holiday, a clock change) used as the hook for a matching trend.
4. **Trending sounds and templates** matched to a concept they fit, only if your business account can use them.
5. **A watch list** with what would move each trend up, and **a skip list** with the reason: peaked, falling, or off-brand.

## What it changes

Nothing. It reads trends and your past posts and writes concepts. It never posts, schedules or replies. X calls bill your own X developer account, so the skill keeps them to a handful per run and tells you how many it made.

## Tips

- Run it on the same day each week, so the slopes compare like with like.
- Keep your seed topics written the way people search ("grow light", not "lighting solutions").
- Paste the TikTok list on the day you run it; ranks move fast.
- A trend on the watch list that holds its slope for a second week is usually a safer bet than this week's spike.
