# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "influencer-vetting" skill. Vet these creators before we sign anyone: check for fake followers, audience fit and brand safety, and rank them.

## What you need

- **Your shortlist.** YouTube: the channel ID, which starts with UC (on the channel page, open the "...more" link under the description, then Share channel, then Copy channel ID); a handle alone can't be looked up. Instagram and TikTok: the handle, the follower count, and stats for the 20 newest posts (date, views, likes, comments, shares on TikTok, and the first line of the caption).
- **Comment samples** for Instagram and TikTok creators: 30 or more, across 3 or more posts, each marked with the date of its post.
- **What the creators shared,** if anything: an audience insights screenshot (countries and ages) and weekly follower counts. Without them, those two checks say "unknown" and you get the questions to send.
- **Your target audience** (countries and ages), your redlines, your competitors, and quoted fees if you have them.
- **Optional: the InsightfulPipe MCP with a YouTube channel of yours connected.** The skill then reads YouTube creators' subscribers, recent uploads, views, likes and comments itself, in about 9 calls for 2 creators. Without it, paste YouTube numbers the same way as Instagram's.

## Example requests

**A full shortlist**
Hey Claude, I just added the "influencer-vetting" skill. Here are three YouTube channels and two Instagram creators for our spring launch. Our buyers are in the US and Canada, aged 25 to 44. Vet and rank them.

**Fake follower check on one creator**
Hey Claude, I just added the "influencer-vetting" skill. This Instagram creator has 60k followers and very high likes. Here are their last 20 posts, 40 comments and their weekly follower counts. Are the followers and the engagement real?

**Before renewing a deal**
Hey Claude, I just added the "influencer-vetting" skill. We want to book this TikTok creator again. Check how their sponsored posts do against their normal ones, and whether they took a deal from a competitor in the last 90 days.

**Brand safety only**
Hey Claude, I just added the "influencer-vetting" skill. Our redlines are alcohol, gambling and politics. Read the last 20 videos on this YouTube channel and tell me if anything touches them.

## What you get

1. **A ranked shortlist,** with a verdict per creator: Book, Book after checks, Hold or Drop.
2. **Eight checks per creator,** each with the number behind it: engagement against the follower tier, reach beyond followers, comment quality, sudden follower jumps, audience country and age fit, brand safety, sponsorship load and competitor deals, and sponsored against organic results.
3. **Questions to send each creator** you might book, to close every open point.
4. **A list of what couldn't be checked,** and why.

## What it can change for you

Nothing. This skill only reads. It never contacts a creator, comments or posts.

## Tips

- Ask creators for their insights screenshot early. Audience country and age live only in the creator's own insights, so no tool can check them without it.
- A flag is a reason to look closer, not proof of fraud. Read the evidence line before you decide.
- Have past campaign results? Give the skill your own engagement medians and it will use them instead of its starting references.
- Run it again a month after a campaign to see whether a creator's numbers held.
