# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "influencer-campaign-manager" skill. Find YouTube creators who could promote my product, vet them, and draft the brief and outreach.

## What you need

- **The InsightfulPipe MCP connected to Claude, with DataForSEO added.** The skill searches YouTube and reads video stats and comments itself, so you don't paste follower counts.
- **Google Analytics 4,** strongly recommended. It sets your baseline and measures the campaign afterwards.
- **Your product, landing page and goal,** plus three searches your buyers make. Without the searches, the skill suggests them from your product.
- **Your budget or the agreed fees,** if you have them. Without them, the plan shows budget shares and the cost metrics wait for the fees.
- **For creator mode:** your YouTube channel (YouTube Analytics) or Instagram account connected.
- **For gifting mode:** your creator list, the landed cost of one parcel and, if you know it, what a sponsored post costs you. Nothing needs to be connected; Shopify or WooCommerce, GA4 and YouTube Data make the numbers exact.
- **For co-marketing mode:** your product, buyer, domain and any partner ideas. Nothing needs to be connected; DataForSEO, GA4, Search Console and Klaviyo or Mailchimp measure the overlap for you.

## Example requests

**Find and vet creators**
Hey Claude, I just added the "influencer-campaign-manager" skill. Find YouTube creators who rank for "sprint planning tips" and "best project management app", score them, and tell me who to approach first.

**Brief and outreach**
Hey Claude, I just added the "influencer-campaign-manager" skill. Write the campaign brief, an outreach email and a proposal for the top two creators. Our goal is sign-ups.

**Captions, scripts and a giveaway**
Hey Claude, I just added the "influencer-campaign-manager" skill. Draft the sponsored captions, a 60-second launch script and the rules for a comment giveaway, with the disclosures in place.

**Measure a campaign**
Hey Claude, I just added the "influencer-campaign-manager" skill. Our creator videos went live last month. Pull the results from GA4 and the videos, and tell me who to renew. I'll give you each creator's fee.

**Creator mode**
Hey Claude, I just added the "influencer-campaign-manager" skill. Write my media kit description from my YouTube channel's last 90 days.

**Influencer gifting and product seeding**
Hey Claude, I just added the "influencer-campaign-manager" skill. Here's a list of 60 creators and our parcel cost. Plan a product seeding round: who gets a parcel, how many to send, the note, the follow-ups and one discount code per creator, and tell me afterwards who should get a paid deal.

**Co-marketing partnership**
Hey Claude, I just added the "influencer-campaign-manager" skill. Find companies that sell to our buyer without competing with us, score them, pick the best joint campaign and draft the pitch and a one-page co-marketing proposal.

## What you get

1. **A baseline from your GA4.** What social and video visits are worth to you today, any past influencer links, and whether conversions can be measured.
2. **A shortlist of four creators,** each scored out of 100 with the parts shown: topic fit, engagement, reach efficiency and recency. Each gets a tier, a rating, notes on their description (do they sell their own offer? any sponsorship?) and message fit.
3. **An audience check** on the top two creators' comments, with the questions their viewers ask that your product answers.
4. **Six co-marketing partners:** publishers, complementary tools, events and marketplaces already writing about your topic.
5. **The plan:** phases, deliverables, budget shares and a tracked link for every creator.
6. **The assets:** the brief, outreach email, proposal, disclosed captions, launch and review scripts, giveaway rules and a paid amplification plan.
7. **A measurement plan,** or the results for a campaign that already ran. Measuring uses extra DataForSEO calls (2 per sponsored video at each check), and the skill asks before it runs them.

In **gifting mode** you get a break-even posting rate from your own parcel cost, a seed score for every creator on your list, wave sizes, the ask and parcel note, a follow-up schedule, one code and link per creator, and at day 30 the posting rate, cost per posted piece, orders per code and the creators who earned a paid deal.

In **co-marketing mode** you get partner candidates with competitors set aside, a score out of 100 for each (shared buyer, reach balance, existing traffic, workflow fit), the campaign that fits the evidence, a pitch email, a one-page proposal covering who does what, the lead split and shared tracking, and how to measure it. It uses up to 7 DataForSEO calls and asks first.

## What it can change for you

With your yes on the exact change, it can:
- add a creator tracker tab to your Google Sheet
- add an "Influencer" channel to GA4, so creator traffic shows up as its own channel
- create one discount code per gifted creator in WooCommerce (on Shopify you create them, and the skill reads them back)

It never sends emails, messages or parcels, never shares a contact list, and never posts, schedules content or runs ads.

## Tips

- Pick searches your buyers actually type, not your brand name. The creators who rank for them already have your audience.
- Use one `utm_medium=influencer` for every creator, so the results come back with one filter.
- Treat a score as a starting point. It rests on one video, so watch a few more before you sign anything.
- Gifting: send a first wave, read the posting rate at day 30, and size the next wave from it rather than from a guess.
