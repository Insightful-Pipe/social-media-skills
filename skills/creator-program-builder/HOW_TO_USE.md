# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "creator-program-builder" skill. We've paid a handful of creators one-off this quarter. Score them on real results and turn it into a creator program: who stays, what we pay, how we find more, and what we send them.

## What you need

- **Your creator roster:** each creator's platform, what you paid in the last 90 days, the posts agreed and delivered (links or video ids), their tracking link or promo code, and whether usage rights were agreed. Paste it, upload it, or point to a Google Sheet.
- **The result that counts:** sign-up, purchase, lead, booked call or install.
- **Your target cost per result,** if you have one. Without it, the skill uses the median of your own non-creator Meta or TikTok ads.
- **Your monthly creator budget,** any customers who already post about you (with the plan each is on), and your plan prices, so product credit has a value.
- **Optional, for live numbers:** the InsightfulPipe MCP with YouTube, GA4, Meta Ads, TikTok Ads or Google Sheets connected. With nothing connected, paste each creator's views, visits and sign-ups or code redemptions instead.

## Example requests

**Build the program**
Hey Claude, I just added the "creator-program-builder" skill. Here's our creator roster from the last quarter. Build an always-on creator program from it. Our target is $50 per sign-up and the budget is $3,000 a month.

**Renewal review**
Hey Claude, I just added the "creator-program-builder" skill. Three creator deals end this month. Tell me who to renew, who to raise and who to drop, with the numbers.

**UGC creator program**
Hey Claude, I just added the "creator-program-builder" skill. We run creator videos as partnership ads on Meta. Score each creator on their ad results and tell me whose content we should keep buying rights to.

**Recruiting**
Hey Claude, I just added the "creator-program-builder" skill. Find YouTube channels in our category that could join the program, and tell me what to offer each one per post. Here are three we already know about: Salon Suite Talk, Tiny Clinic Ops and Booth Rent Life.

## What you get

1. **A scorecard per creator:** delivery, disclosure, views, engagement, cost per 1,000 views, visits, results, cost per result, and ad results when their content runs as ads.
2. **A verdict per creator:** core, renew, ads only, probation, drop or not graded yet, with the rule and the numbers behind it.
3. **The program:** tiers, retainers and bonuses with the math shown, monthly quotas, and how it fits your budget.
4. **Recruits:** customers who already post, YouTube channels in your category with a per-post offer each, and who was skipped and why. It sizes the channels you name; a YouTube search for more runs when YouTube's search quota allows.
5. **Rights, disclosure and tracking:** each creator's link, promo code and ad-name prefix, so the next review runs on its own.
6. **The onboarding kit and a review calendar.**

## What it can change for you

With your yes on the exact data, it can add a scorecard tab to your Google Sheet. It never pays creators, messages them, changes ads or posts anything.

## Tips

- Run it every 90 days, when deals come up for renewal, and read the scorecard monthly in between.
- Give every creator the tracking link and code from the plan before their next post; that's what makes the next review accurate.
- For one creator campaign's search, outreach and contract, use `influencer-campaign-manager` if installed. For the creative brief, use `ugc-brief-generator`.
