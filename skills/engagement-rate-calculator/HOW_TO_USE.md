# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "engagement-rate-calculator" skill. Work out our Instagram engagement rate every way it can be calculated and tell me whether it's good.

## What you need

- **Either** the InsightfulPipe MCP connected to Claude with your social profiles added (Instagram, Facebook Pages, LinkedIn Pages, TikTok, X, YouTube or Pinterest). The skill pulls posts, reach, views, impressions and followers itself.
- **Or** no connection at all: paste or attach one row per post (date, format, likes, comments, shares, saves, and reach, views or impressions) plus your follower count. For a creator you don't own, the likes, comments, views and followers on their public profile are enough.
- **The previous period,** if you want to see the trend. The skill compares your account with itself before it compares it with anyone else.

## Example requests

**Is our rate good?**
Hey Claude, I just added the "engagement-rate-calculator" skill. What is a good engagement rate on Instagram for an account our size, and how does ours compare?

**Two reports disagree**
Hey Claude, I just added the "engagement-rate-calculator" skill. Our agency says our engagement rate is 5% and an online calculator says 0.6%. Which one is right?

**One post**
Hey Claude, I just added the "engagement-rate-calculator" skill. Did yesterday's carousel beat our usual engagement rate?

**A creator**
Hey Claude, I just added the "engagement-rate-calculator" skill. Here are the last 12 posts from a creator we might pay. Work out their engagement rate by followers and by views.

**Every network**
Hey Claude, I just added the "engagement-rate-calculator" skill. Calculate our engagement rate on Instagram, LinkedIn and TikTok for the last 90 days.

## What you get

1. **A straight answer** to the question you asked, naming the formula it rests on.
2. **Every formula side by side:** by reach, by views, by impressions, by followers and per day, plus the account rate and the median and mean per post, each with its numerator and denominator.
3. **Your account against itself:** the same rates for the previous period.
4. **Benchmark comparisons that match your formula,** each with its source, publication date, statistic and the interactions it counts, and a verdict: above, in line or below.
5. **Your best and weakest posts,** and the rate by format when posts carry one.
6. **What it couldn't grade,** and why: fewer than 10 posts, a missing connection, or no fair benchmark.

## Tips

- Give it at least 10 posts. Under that, it shows the numbers but won't call them good or bad.
- If you compare your rate with a number from a blog or a calculator, ask what it divides by. The skill will tell you which of your rates matches it.
- It reads only. It never posts, edits or deletes anything.
