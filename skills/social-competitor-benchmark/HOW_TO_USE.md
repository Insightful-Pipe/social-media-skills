# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "social-competitor-benchmark" skill. Compare our social accounts with our three main competitors over the last 90 days and tell me which formats we should test.

## What you need

- **Your rivals' handles on each network,** up to five rivals. The skill won't guess a handle from a company name.
- **The InsightfulPipe MCP,** if you want the numbers pulled for you. Your own Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, YouTube, X, TikTok and Pinterest accounts are read live; rival YouTube channels and X accounts are read live too, and rival Instagram Business accounts when Instagram is connected with Facebook login. Rivals' ads come from the public Meta, TikTok and LinkedIn ad libraries.
- **Nothing else, if you'd rather paste.** A table with one row per post (or per account) from native exports or public profiles is enough, and every pasted figure is marked in the report.
- **Earlier follower counts,** if you have them, to show rival growth.

## Example requests

**Full benchmark**
Hey Claude, I just added the "social-competitor-benchmark" skill. Benchmark our Instagram, LinkedIn, YouTube, X and TikTok against Competitor A, B and C for the last 90 days.

**One network**
Hey Claude, I just added the "social-competitor-benchmark" skill. Compare our Instagram account with these four accounts. Here are their handles; I'll paste their post stats.

**What rivals pay to push**
Hey Claude, I just added the "social-competitor-benchmark" skill. Which of our competitors' organic posts are they also running as ads, and on which networks?

**Template for a client**
Hey Claude, I just added the "social-competitor-benchmark" skill. Fill a social media competitor analysis for my client from this pasted table, and mark which numbers came from the client.

## What you get

1. **A scoreboard per network:** followers, growth, posts per week, top format, median engagements and median engagement rate per post, with the source of every number.
2. **Format mix** for every account, and the formats worth testing.
3. **Each rival's top posts and the themes** that work for them.
4. **Paid beside organic:** each rival's ads in the Meta and TikTok libraries (active now) and the LinkedIn library (shown in the window, since it can't filter by status), and the organic posts they also run as ads, every matching post listed when the same text ran on more than one network.
5. **Up to five tests,** each with what to post, how many, and the number that counts as success.
6. **What couldn't be compared,** and how to fill it next time.

## What it can change for you

Nothing. The skill only reads your accounts and the public ad libraries.

## Tips

- Run it every quarter and keep the scoreboard; the next run uses it to show rival growth.
- On X, reads are billed to your own X developer account. The skill tells you the estimate before reading a lot of posts.
- A rival with fewer than 8 posts on a network is listed but not ranked, so a quiet account can't top the table on two lucky posts.
