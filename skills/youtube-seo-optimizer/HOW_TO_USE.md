# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "youtube-seo-optimizer" skill. Audit my top YouTube videos and rewrite the title, description, chapters and tags for the one with the most to gain.

## What you need

- **The InsightfulPipe MCP connected to Claude, with your YouTube channel added.** The skill reads your videos, views and traffic sources itself; you don't need to export anything.
- **DataForSEO in the same workspace,** for keyword research, the live YouTube results and video transcripts. One optimized video uses about 5 DataForSEO calls.
- **The video you care about,** if you have one in mind. Without it, the skill audits your 10 most-viewed videos from the last 28 days and picks the one with the most to gain.
- **Your country and language,** if they aren't the United States and English.

## Example requests

**Audit the channel**
Hey Claude, I just added the "youtube-seo-optimizer" skill. Score the titles, descriptions and tags of my 10 most-viewed videos this month and tell me what to fix first.

**Optimize one video**
Hey Claude, I just added the "youtube-seo-optimizer" skill. Rewrite the title, description and tags for my video "How to Bake Sourdough Bread at Home" from search data.

**Keyword and competitor research**
Hey Claude, I just added the "youtube-seo-optimizer" skill. Research the YouTube keyword for my latest tutorial and show me which title words and tags the same-topic videos in the top 20 use that mine doesn't.

**Chapters**
Hey Claude, I just added the "youtube-seo-optimizer" skill. Write chapters for this video from its transcript, checked against YouTube's chapter rules.

**Topic ideas**
Hey Claude, I just added the "youtube-seo-optimizer" skill. Find video topics people search for around "sourdough bread" that my channel hasn't covered yet.

## What you get

1. **An audit table:** a metadata score for each video, with its views, its share of views from YouTube search, and the checks that failed.
2. **Channel patterns:** the problems that repeat across your videos, such as missing chapters or titles that get cut off.
3. **Keyword research:** Google demand, YouTube interest and the live YouTube results for the video's topic, with the primary keyword and the rule that picked it.
4. **A competitor gap:** the title words and tags that same-topic videos ranking for the keyword use and yours doesn't.
5. **A rewrite:** three title options, a new description opening, chapters from the transcript and a tag list within YouTube's limit, with the score before and after.
6. **Topic ideas** your channel hasn't covered.
7. **The exact update,** ready to apply, with your current title, description and tags kept for rollback.

## What it can change for you

With your yes on the exact change, it can apply the new title, description and tags to one video at a time. It reads the video again afterwards to show you what changed. It never touches thumbnails, privacy, playlists or comments.

## Tips

- Pick the title option that's truest to the video. A title that over-promises loses viewers in the first seconds.
- If you already have a transcript, paste it in. The skill then skips the paid transcript call.
- Run the audit again a few weeks after changes, and compare the search share of the videos you updated.
