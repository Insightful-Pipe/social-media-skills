# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "facebook-page-analyst" skill. Give me last month's report for my Facebook Page and tell me the three things to do next.

## What you need

- **The InsightfulPipe MCP connected to Claude, with your Facebook Page added.** The skill pulls the data itself; you don't need to export anything.
- **Your time zone,** if you want posting times in your local time. Without it, the skill uses UTC.
- **What the page is for:** reach, engagement or clicks to your website. Without it, posts are ranked by views.

## Example requests

**Monthly report**
Hey Claude, I just added the "facebook-page-analyst" skill. Build last month's Facebook Page report and compare it with the month before.

**Why did reach drop?**
Hey Claude, I just added the "facebook-page-analyst" skill. My Facebook Page views fell over the last few weeks. Show me the trend and explain what caused it.

**Best posts and formats**
Hey Claude, I just added the "facebook-page-analyst" skill. Rank my top Facebook posts from the last 90 days and tell me whether Reels, videos, photos or links work best.

**When to post**
Hey Claude, I just added the "facebook-page-analyst" skill. When should I post on my Facebook Page? I'm in New York time.

**Comments and replies**
Hey Claude, I just added the "facebook-page-analyst" skill. Check for angry reactions, hidden comments and unanswered comments on my page, and draft replies for me to approve.

## What you get

1. **The month in numbers:** views, viewers, engagements, profile views, follows and posts, compared with the month before, with a clear call on which changes are real.
2. **Three things to do next,** each with the numbers behind it.
3. **Eight modules:** monthly report, reach trend, top posts leaderboard, post type comparison, best time to post, followers, negative feedback and hides, and response time.
4. **Honest gaps:** where Meta returns too little data (for example, follower locations on pages under 100 followers), the report says "not graded" or "unknown" instead of guessing. A post Meta returns no views for is left out of the medians and listed, never counted as zero.

## What it can change for you

With your yes on the exact change, it can:
- reply to comments, using replies you approve one by one
- hide spam or abusive comments
- unhide a comment hidden by mistake

It never publishes, edits or deletes posts.

## Tips

- Run it at the start of each month for the monthly report.
- Messenger response time isn't available through the connector; the skill measures how fast the page answers public comments instead.
- Small pages: best time and post type results need at least 20 posts, so the skill looks back up to a year when the last 90 days are too thin.
