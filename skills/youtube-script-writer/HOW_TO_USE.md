# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "youtube-script-writer" skill. Write my next YouTube video script around the points where my viewers usually leave, and cut it into Shorts.

## What you need

- **A topic and what the viewer should be able to do by the end.** That's the only thing the skill can't default.
- **Optional, and better: the InsightfulPipe MCP connected with YouTube and DataForSEO.** The skill then reads your own retention curves, video lengths and chapters, and checks which phrases are worth ranking for. You don't export anything.
- **With nothing connected:** paste a past transcript, an outline, or the retention readings from YouTube Studio (the share still watching at 0:30 and where the dips are). The script still works; the leave points are then marked as general practice rather than your channel's.
- **If the video is sponsored:** the brand, its approved talking points and the read length it asked for.

## Example requests

**A new long-form script**
Hey Claude, I just added the "youtube-script-writer" skill. Write a 10-minute script on how to repot a fiddle leaf fig, for my channel, talking head with B-roll.

**A YouTube script template for my channel**
Hey Claude, I just added the "youtube-script-writer" skill. Look at where viewers leave my last few tutorials and give me a script template I can reuse for every video.

**Sponsor placement**
Hey Claude, I just added the "youtube-script-writer" skill. I have a 60-second sponsor read in my next video. Where should it go so I lose the fewest viewers?

**Review my draft**
Hey Claude, I just added the "youtube-script-writer" skill. Here's my draft script. Time it at my speaking pace and tell me what to fix before I film.

**Shorts from a long video**
Hey Claude, I just added the "youtube-script-writer" skill. Turn this script into three Shorts that work on their own.

## What you get

1. **A leave map:** how many of your viewers are still watching at 0:30, where your past videos lose people (by chapter), what you were saying at the deepest dip, how much your sponsor reads cost, and how many leave during the outro.
2. **The phrases to rank for,** from the top YouTube results and Google search volumes, with the reason for the main one.
3. **3 title options,** each with a thumbnail idea.
4. **A timed script:** every beat with its start time, spoken words, B-roll, on-screen text and pattern change, with re-hooks before your usual leave points and the sponsor read placed from your data.
5. **Chapters** ready to paste into the description.
6. **2 to 3 Shorts** cut from the script, each with its own hook.
7. **A checklist** with pass or fix for each item.

The sample on this page is invented: a bike repair shop's channel writing a 6-minute video on squeaky disc brakes. Its past tutorials lose viewers at the subscribe ask in the intro, at the tools list and during sponsor reads (2.5 times faster than the rest), so the new script drops the intro ask, shrinks the tools list behind a re-hook, and moves a shorter sponsor read to after the second fix.

## What it changes

Nothing. It reads channel data and search results and writes the script. It never uploads, retitles or edits a video.

## Tips

- Give it a transcript of a past video with timestamps. The skill measures your speaking pace from it, so the timings match how you really talk.
- Run it again after the video has a few hundred views, and compare the new curve with the leave map.
- For titles, descriptions and tags in depth, pair it with a YouTube SEO skill.
