# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "customer-ugc-program" skill. Build a customer UGC program for our Instagram: find what customers already posted, fix when we ask for photos, and tell me if customer posts beat ours.

## What you need

- **Nothing connected is fine.** Paste your last 90 days of posts with their reach, likes, comments, saves and shares, your Instagram Tagged tab, a few order and ship dates, and when your post-purchase email asks for photos.
- **Better: the InsightfulPipe MCP connected to Claude,** with Instagram, your Facebook Page, Shopify and Klaviyo added. The skill then pulls your posts, order dates and email numbers itself.
- **Your Tagged tab and Story mentions.** The Instagram connector can't read tagged posts or mentions, so paste the Tagged tab. Story mentions disappear after 24 hours, so check them daily in the app.
- **Answers to a few questions:** your product type, how many days shipping takes, which email asks for photos and when, and whether you want to use customer content in ads.

## Example requests

**Start a program**
Hey Claude, I just added the "customer-ugc-program" skill. We sell candles on Shopify. Build us a user generated content strategy that brings in a few customer photos every week.

**Asking permission the right way**
Hey Claude, I just added the "customer-ugc-program" skill. How do I ask permission to repost a customer's Instagram photo? Write the comment, the DM and a consent log I can keep in a Google Sheet.

**Fix the timing of the ask**
Hey Claude, I just added the "customer-ugc-program" skill. Our post-purchase email asks for photos 5 days after the order. Check our Shopify fulfilment dates and tell me when it should go out.

**Is it worth it?**
Hey Claude, I just added the "customer-ugc-program" skill. Compare our reposted customer photos with our own posts over the last 90 days, by format.

## What you get

1. **Found content:** what customers already posted, ranked by engagement, with what was skipped and why.
2. **The ask timing** from your own order and fulfilment dates, and where else to ask: packaging insert, Stories, comments and DMs.
3. **A volume plan:** how many reposts your feed needs each month and where the gap is, labelled as an estimate.
4. **Permission templates** with separate scopes for reposts, your website and email, and paid ads.
5. **A consent log** that gates every repost, plus a check of the log you already keep.
6. **A repost calendar** on your best weekday.
7. **Customer vs brand content,** compared on your own reach and engagement, by format.

## What it can change for you

With your yes on the exact payload, it can:
- write the consent log to a new tab in your Google Sheet
- repost one customer post on Instagram, only when the log shows their yes

It never messages customers, edits your Klaviyo flows or puts a customer's content in ads.

## Tips

- Use one credit line on every repost, "Photo: @handle". The next run finds your customer posts by it.
- Rerun it every quarter: the measurement gets better as reposts pile up.
- Ask for ads use separately. A yes to a repost is not a yes to an ad.
