# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "social-listening-monitor" skill. Run last week's social listening report for my brand against my top three competitors, and tell me what we need to answer.

## What you need

- **Your brand terms, rivals and category phrases.** The brand name and handle, up to 4 competitors, and up to 5 phrases buyers use (for example "salon booking app").
- **Either a paste or a connection.** Paste a mention list or an alerts CSV export (date, platform, author, text, link, engagement), or connect X organic, YouTube, DataForSEO, Facebook Pages and Instagram to the InsightfulPipe MCP and the skill pulls the data itself.
- **Facts the replies may use (optional).** For example a known outage and its status, your support route, and where common settings live. Anything you don't give is left in [square brackets] in the drafts for you to fill in, never guessed.
- **Last weeks' counts lines,** if you ran it before without a connection. They build the 8-week baseline that spikes are measured against.

## Example requests

**Weekly report**
Hey Claude, I just added the "social-listening-monitor" skill. Give me last week's social listening report for our brand: volume, share of voice against Competitor A and Competitor B, sentiment and anything we need to reply to.

**From an export**
Hey Claude, I just added the "social-listening-monitor" skill. Here is our alerts CSV for the last 9 weeks. Tell me if this week is a spike, what people are unhappy about, and draft replies to the worst complaints.

**A rival had a bad week**
Hey Claude, I just added the "social-listening-monitor" skill. Competitor B just raised prices. Find the people on X and Reddit asking for alternatives and draft replies I can post.

**Reply for me**
Hey Claude, I just added the "social-listening-monitor" skill. Run the report, then reply to the unanswered complaints on our Facebook and Instagram posts. Show me each reply before you post it.

## What you get

1. **Volume and spikes:** this week against the 8-week average for your brand and each rival on each platform. A spike needs more than twice the average and at least 5 mentions.
2. **Share of voice** on X, YouTube and Reddit, this week against the baseline, read next to sentiment so a complaint wave isn't mistaken for a win.
3. **Sentiment** per platform with the quote and link behind every label, and the themes people keep raising.
4. **Needs an answer:** complaints and questions, own-page and unanswered first, with draft replies for the top 10 and a list of the facts to fill in before posting.
5. **Worth joining:** people asking for a recommendation or leaving a rival in the last 14 days, with draft replies for the top 5.
6. **A counts line** to paste next week, so the baseline keeps building even without a connection.

## What it can do for you

With your yes on each exact reply, it can post:
- replies on X to posts that tag your handle
- replies to comments on your Facebook page and Instagram posts
- replies to YouTube comment threads

Reddit, TikTok and anything that doesn't tag your handle on X stay drafts for you to post by hand. It never likes, follows, hides, deletes or sends direct messages.

## Tips

- Run it early on Monday for the week that just ended: X's recent search reaches back exactly 7 days from the moment it runs. Run it later and the first days of the week have no X posts read; the report names those days.
- X bills each post read to your own X developer account, so the skill reads 10 posts per term and asks before reading more. The report shows the posts read and the estimated bill.
- Add exclusion words if your brand name is a common word, so off-topic posts are dropped instead of scored.
