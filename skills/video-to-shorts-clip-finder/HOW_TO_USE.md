# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "video-to-shorts-clip-finder" skill. Here's the transcript of our latest YouTube video. Turn it into Shorts and Reels: pick the best clips with timestamps and write the hook, captions and crop notes for each.

## What you need

- **A transcript with timestamps.** From YouTube (Show transcript, or YouTube Studio > Subtitles > download), an SRT or VTT file, or the export from Zoom, Riverside, Descript or your editor. Plain text with no times can't be clipped, and the skill will ask for a timed version.
- **Who is on screen when,** if it changes: one speaker, two people side by side, or a screen share, with rough time ranges. Without it, the crop notes assume one speaker.
- **Where the clips go,** if not YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels and TikTok.
- **How long the clips can run.** Without it, 15 to 60 seconds.
- **When to post:** start date, posts per week, the time and your time zone. Without it, three a week from next Monday at noon.
- **Optional: the InsightfulPipe MCP with your YouTube channel added.** The skill then reads the video's own retention curve, so it can see where viewers rewatched, jumped to or left. Add the Social Media Scheduler too and it plans around posts already in your queue, and can save the clips as drafts once you say yes.

## Example requests

**YouTube video to Shorts**
Hey Claude, I just added the "video-to-shorts-clip-finder" skill. Turn our latest long YouTube video into Shorts. Use the retention curve to find the moments people rewatched.

**Podcast clips for social media**
Hey Claude, I just added the "video-to-shorts-clip-finder" skill. Here's the SRT from this week's podcast recording. Find 6 clips for Instagram Reels, TikTok and LinkedIn, with captions for each.

**Webinar replay**
Hey Claude, I just added the "video-to-shorts-clip-finder" skill. Our 45-minute webinar has a screen-share demo in the middle. Pick clips up to 2 minutes long and tell the editor how to crop each one for vertical.

**Weeks of Reels from one livestream**
Hey Claude, I just added the "video-to-shorts-clip-finder" skill. Plan three weeks of Reels from this livestream, three a week at 6 p.m. Chicago time, and skip days that already have something in our scheduler.

**Clips, then drafts**
Hey Claude, I just added the "video-to-shorts-clip-finder" skill. The cut files are in our media library now. Save the clips you planned as drafts in the scheduler. Show me every post first.

## What you get

1. **A header** with the video, its length, the transcript source, and whether a retention curve was used.
2. **A clip table:** 5 to 10 clips (fewer when fewer are good enough), each with timestamps, length, chapter and scores.
3. **A card per clip:** where to cut, the opening line, a cold-open suggestion when a stronger line sits later, a Shorts title, a caption per network with its character count, on-screen text, crop notes for 9:16, and a pass or fail check against each network's length and caption limits.
4. **A posting plan:** date, time and networks for each clip, with no two clips from the same part of the video on back-to-back posting days.
5. **What was left out,** and the rule that dropped each moment.
6. **Draft posts,** ready to save, marked "not run" until you confirm.

## What it can change for you

With your yes on the exact list, and once the cut clip files exist, it can save each clip as a draft post in the InsightfulPipe Social Media Scheduler for YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, Facebook and X. Nothing is published or scheduled live; you publish the drafts yourself. The skill doesn't edit or cut video.

## Tips

- Give it the whole transcript, not highlights. The best clips are often in the middle, where nobody remembers them.
- If guests speak in the clips, check they're happy for clips to be posted.
- In YouTube Studio, set each Short's related video to the full video so viewers can move from the clip to the whole talk.
- Run it again a few weeks after posting, with a newer retention curve, to find the next batch.
