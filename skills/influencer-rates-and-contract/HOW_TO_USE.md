# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "influencer-rates-and-contract" skill. How much should I pay this creator, and write the deal terms.

## What you need

- **The creator's recent post views,** at least 5 per format, with dates. Paste them from their insights or media kit. For a YouTube creator, the channel id or a link to one of their videos is enough when the InsightfulPipe MCP is connected: the skill reads the views itself.
- **What you want:** the deliverables, and which rights (organic reuse, paid usage, whitelisting, exclusivity) for how many months.
- **Your past creator deals,** if you have them: fee, views at day 30 and results. They set your own rate per 1,000 views and the value cap. Without them, the skill uses labelled default rates.
- **A target cost per result and a budget,** if you have them.

## Example requests

**Price an offer**
Hey Claude, I just added the "influencer-rates-and-contract" skill. What should we offer this YouTube creator for one integration and one Short? Here's their channel id. We want 3 months of paid usage.

**Several creators, one budget**
Hey Claude, I just added the "influencer-rates-and-contract" skill. Price these three Instagram creators from the Reel views I pasted, and tell me what to cut to stay under $5,000.

**Check a draft contract**
Hey Claude, I just added the "influencer-rates-and-contract" skill. The creator's agency sent this contract. Price the usage rights they're asking for and flag anything I should show a lawyer.

**Creator rate card**
Hey Claude, I just added the "influencer-rates-and-contract" skill. I'm a creator. Here are my last 15 TikTok views and my last three paid deals. Build my rate card and the terms I send with every quote.

## What you get

1. **Expected views per format,** from the creator's recent posts (priced on their sponsored posts when those get fewer views), next to their follower count.
2. **A fee ladder for each deliverable:** opening offer, target and walk-away, with the rate per 1,000 views and where it came from.
3. **A value cap** from your own results, and the deal structure: flat fee, a base plus a views bonus, or a base plus a per-result bonus.
4. **Rights and terms priced line by line:** organic reuse, paid usage, whitelisting, exclusivity, extra approval rounds and rush, by term length.
5. **A term sheet per creator:** deliverables, timeline, approvals, tracking, disclosure, claims, rights, exclusivity, fee, payment and kill fee.
6. **Lawyer flags** for the clauses that need one, and a budget check with what to cut first.

## What it changes

Nothing. It reads public YouTube data and writes text.

## Tips

- Give past deals in the same format: two or more set your own rate, and the report says which fees still rest on defaults.
- Ask for day-30 insights screenshots in every deal. They feed the next price.
- A term sheet isn't a contract. Agree it first, then have a lawyer write or check the contract.
