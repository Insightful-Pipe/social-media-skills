# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "tiktok-organic-analyst" skill. Analyse my TikTok account, tell me what's working, and give me a strategy, ideas and scripts for next month.

## What you need

- **The InsightfulPipe MCP connected to Claude, with your TikTok account added.** The skill pulls your profile and every public video itself; you don't need screenshots or exports.
- **What the account is for,** if it isn't clear from your bio.
- **Optional: retention numbers from TikTok Studio.** The connection has no watch time, so paste average watch time or "watched full video" for a few videos if you want retention included.

## Example requests

**Full analysis**
Hey Claude, I just added the "tiktok-organic-analyst" skill. Analyse my TikTok and tell me which hooks, topics and lengths work best.

**Monthly report**
Hey Claude, I just added the "tiktok-organic-analyst" skill. Write my TikTok monthly report for last month and compare it with the month before.

**Ideas and scripts**
Hey Claude, I just added the "tiktok-organic-analyst" skill. Give me 10 TikTok video ideas and 3 scripts based on my best videos.

**Followers**
Hey Claude, I just added the "tiktok-organic-analyst" skill. I had 250 followers on 1 September. What's driving my follower growth?

## What you get

1. **A grade out of 100,** with the math shown, across momentum, posting consistency, shareability, hooks and length.
2. **Your top 5 actions,** each with the videos and numbers behind it.
3. **Hook, length, topic and hashtag tables** measured against your own median video.
4. **A monthly report** for the last complete month against the one before.
5. **A content strategy:** pillars, hooks to use and drop, length and posting pace.
6. **10 video ideas and 3 timed scripts,** each modelled on one of your real top videos.
7. **A list of what couldn't be measured,** and why.

## What it can change for you

Nothing. It only reads your account and never posts. You film and post the scripts yourself.

## Tips

- Run it monthly. It reports today's follower count but doesn't store it, so give it that number next time to measure growth.
- Videos under 7 days old are listed but not graded, because they are still collecting views.
- If you also run TikTok ads, analyse them separately: this skill covers organic videos only.
