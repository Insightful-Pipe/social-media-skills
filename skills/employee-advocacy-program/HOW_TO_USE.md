# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "employee-advocacy-program" skill. Help me start an employee advocacy program on LinkedIn: pick the topics and the first people, and write the guidelines and this week's post kit.

## What you need

- **A list of volunteers:** name, role, team, roughly how often they post on LinkedIn, and their connections band. Only people who said yes.
- **Your goal:** sign-ups, demo requests, hiring or awareness.
- **Facts people may use:** product facts, published customer results and links. The kit quotes only these.
- **Optional, the InsightfulPipe MCP** with your LinkedIn company page and GA4 added. The skill then reads your page's posts and the visits each person's link sent. Without it, paste your page's post stats and your analytics rows instead.

## Example requests

**Start a program**
Hey Claude, I just added the "employee-advocacy-program" skill. We want 6 people posting on LinkedIn about our product. Here are our 14 volunteers. Pick the first 6, choose the topics from our company page, and give me the guidelines, links and first kit.

**Review a pilot**
Hey Claude, I just added the "employee-advocacy-program" skill. Our 4-week pilot just ended. Here are everyone's post stats. Who is on target, who needs a nudge, and which posts should we boost?

**Weekly kit**
Hey Claude, I just added the "employee-advocacy-program" skill. Write next week's post kit for our 10 advocates. Our new feature launches on Tuesday.

**Add X**
Hey Claude, I just added the "employee-advocacy-program" skill. Two of our advocates are active on X. Give them X links and tell me how to track those posts too.

## What you get

1. **Starter pillars:** your company page's themes ranked by median engagement rate, with the top 3 picked.
2. **The cohort:** each volunteer's fit score (audience, activity, network), and who starts or joins next.
3. **One-page guidelines** you can send as is, including the "say you work here" disclosure.
4. **A tracked link per person** (utm_content names the person), so analytics shows whose posts sent visits and sign-ups.
5. **A weekly kit of prompt cards:** an angle, a question only that person can answer, a fact they may use and a format idea. Never one post for everyone to paste.
6. **A review scorecard:** posts, impressions, engagement, link visits and sign-ups per person, participation against your target, private nudges, and posts worth boosting as Thought Leader Ads.

## What it can post for you

With a Read & Write connection and your yes on the exact text, it can publish a post you wrote or edited to your own LinkedIn profile. It never posts for anyone else.

## Tips

- Start with 6 people, not the whole company. Each review names the next 4 volunteers to add. If participation stays under your target for 2 reviews in a row, lower the ask to 1 post in 4 weeks before you add more people.
- Keep per-person numbers between you and each person unless they agree to share them.
- Check each person's link once in a browser before the first kit goes out.
- Run the review every 4 weeks, and the kit every week.
