# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "community-strategy-builder" skill. Score our community for the last 30 days and give me a 90-day plan to get members answering each other.

## What you need

- **A short description** of your members, your one goal for the community, and where your audience already spends time (Slack, Discord, Facebook groups, Telegram, a forum).
- **Your team:** who can host, moderate and answer, how many hours a week, and their time zones.
- **For the scorecard, an export** with members (id, join date, staff or not) and messages (id, channel, author, time, and the parent message for replies). Slack, Discord, Circle, Telegram and forum exports all work. Without one, you get the plan, and the scorecard starts after your first month.
- **Optional, the InsightfulPipe MCP** with Slack and GA4 added. The skill then reads your Slack channels, messages and members itself, and pulls the visits and sign-ups your community links sent. With Telegram added, it can post and pin your house rules after you approve them.

## Example requests

**Plan a new community**
Hey Claude, I just added the "community-strategy-builder" skill. We want a community for our customers. They're mostly small agency owners who live in Slack and LinkedIn. Pick the platform and write the channels, welcome path, rules and the first 90 days.

**Monthly scorecard**
Hey Claude, I just added the "community-strategy-builder" skill. Here's our Discord export for October. Score participation, new-member retention and reply speed, and tell me what to fix first.

**Should we move platforms?**
Hey Claude, I just added the "community-strategy-builder" skill. Our Slack community has gone quiet and someone suggested Circle. Score both against where our members actually are, and tell me if a move is worth it.

**Tie it to sign-ups**
Hey Claude, I just added the "community-strategy-builder" skill. Tag every link we post in the community and show me in GA4 how many sign-ups it sends.

## What you get

1. **A platform pick** with the score for each option and one sentence on why.
2. **A scorecard** for participation ratio, 30-day new-member retention, median time to first reply and the share of questions answered within 24 hours, each with the count behind it and a pass, warn or fail against the thresholds or your own targets.
3. **Up to 3 fixes,** each tied to a number, with an owner and a start date.
4. **What sits behind the numbers:** when questions wait, whether quick replies to introductions keep new members, who does the answering, and which channels earn their place.
5. **What the community sends:** visits, sign-ups and key events from tagged links in GA4, mis-tagged links to fix, and churn for members and non-members.
6. **The plan:** channel layout, welcome path and message, house rules, roles and a reply rota, a dated 90-day programme with day 30, 60 and 90 targets, and a link table.

## What it can change for you

With your yes on the exact text or name, and a Read & Write connection:
- create a Slack channel from the layout
- post the welcome message or the weekly thread in Slack
- post and pin the house rules in a Telegram group

It never archives channels, invites or removes members, or messages members directly.

## Tips

- Run it on the same day each month, with the same staff list and question channels, so the trend means something.
- Paste last month's scorecard numbers if you have them; the report compares against your own 3-month median.
- On Slack's free plan, export every month: only the last 90 days of messages stay visible.
