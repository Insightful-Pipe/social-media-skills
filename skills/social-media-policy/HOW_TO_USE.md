# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "social-media-policy" skill. Write a social media policy for my business, with employee guidelines, community guidelines and who approves what.

## What you need

- **A few facts about the business:** what it sells, where it operates, how many staff, which social accounts it runs and who on the team posts, moderates and approves.
- **Your industry,** if it is finance, health or alcohol. The pack then adds that industry's checklist.
- **Optional: the InsightfulPipe MCP with your Facebook Page or Instagram account added.** The skill then reads 90 days of your comments to build the moderation rules and keyword filter, and sets reply targets from how fast your team replies today. Without a connection, paste 20 to 50 recent comments or describe the ones you get most.

## Example requests

**Full policy pack**
Hey Claude, I just added the "social-media-policy" skill. We're a 4-clinic dental group in the US with a Facebook Page and Instagram. Write our social media policy and calibrate the moderation rules on our real comments.

**Employee guidelines only**
Hey Claude, I just added the "social-media-policy" skill. Write social media guidelines for our 60 employees, including what they must disclose when they post about our products.

**Community guidelines**
Hey Claude, I just added the "social-media-policy" skill. Write the community guidelines we pin on our page, and the internal rules for what moderators hide, answer or escalate.

**Response times**
Hey Claude, I just added the "social-media-policy" skill. How fast do we reply to comments today, and what reply targets and escalation steps should go into our policy?

## What you get

1. **What your comments show:** the mix of questions, complaints, spam, abuse and personal details, how consistently each is hidden today, and the patterns that earn their own rule.
2. **A keyword filter list** for Facebook's blocklist and Instagram's hidden words, built only from words that never appear in genuine comments.
3. **Reply targets and an escalation matrix** set from your measured reply times.
4. **The policy pack:** account access and passwords, approval workflow, public community guidelines, moderation rules, employee guidelines and disclosure rules for sponsored posts and staff.
5. **An industry checklist** for finance, health or alcohol, when it applies.
6. **A legal review list:** every clause a lawyer should check. The pack is not legal advice.

## What it can change for you

With your yes on the exact list, it can hide visible spam, scam, abuse and personal-data comments on Facebook and Instagram, and unhide complaints that were hidden by mistake. It never hides complaints or questions, never deletes comments and never bans anyone.

## Tips

- Have a lawyer or compliance officer read the items on the legal review list before you adopt the pack.
- Rerun it every six months: the reply targets move as your team gets faster.
- Pin the community guidelines on each page, so a hidden comment points to a rule people could read.
