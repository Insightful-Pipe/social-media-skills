# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "social-giveaway-planner" skill. Plan a giveaway for our Instagram and check the entry rules before we post it.

## What you need

- **Nothing to start.** Describe the prize, the networks and the dates, or paste your comments, and the skill works from that.
- **Better: the InsightfulPipe MCP connected to Claude,** with your Instagram account, Facebook Page or X account added. Then the skill reads your follower counts and the entries itself: Facebook Page comments, plus Instagram comments and X replies where your connection allows them (some Instagram connections don't expose comments yet, and then it asks for a comment export). Add Shopify and GA4 to measure orders and traffic against the weeks before launch.
- **Your prize's retail value and what it costs you,** and a target cost per follower if you have one.

## Example requests

**Plan and check the rules**
Hey Claude, I just added the "social-giveaway-planner" skill. We want to give away a $200 bundle on Instagram and Facebook. Our idea is "follow, tag 3 friends and share to your Story". Is that allowed, and what should the official rules say?

**Pick the winners from the comments**
Hey Claude, I just added the "social-giveaway-planner" skill. Our Instagram giveaway closed last night. Pick 2 winners and 2 alternates from the comments, and leave out our staff accounts.

**A pasted list (TikTok or any network)**
Hey Claude, I just added the "social-giveaway-planner" skill. Here's the comment export from our TikTok giveaway. Remove duplicates and bots and draw one winner I can prove was random.

**Was it worth it?**
Hey Claude, I just added the "social-giveaway-planner" skill. Our giveaway closed 30 days ago. How many followers did we keep, and what did each one cost?

## What you get

1. **A plan:** the entry mechanic for your goal, a rules check of every part of it on every network (ok, caution or avoid, with the source), the official rules with [brackets] for what only you or your lawyer can fill, questions for your lawyer, launch posts, tagged links and a code per network.
2. **A clean draw:** counts after every cleaning step, each removed entry with its reason, accounts flagged but kept, and a draw record (list fingerprint, seed and method). The entry list stays private, so anyone you give it to, such as an independent checker, can rerun the draw and get the same winners.
3. **A 30-day read:** followers kept above your normal growth on each network, engagement per post before and after, store orders and social traffic per day against the 28 days before launch, giveaway-code orders, and the cost per follower kept.

## What it can do for you

With your yes on the exact text, it can reply to each winning comment on Instagram or Facebook, or to the winning reply on X. It never publishes the giveaway posts, never sends direct messages, and never hides or deletes comments.

## Tips

- Run the Plan stage at least 14 days before launch, so it can record the follower trend the 30-day read needs.
- On X, ask it to collect the replies every few days during the giveaway: X's search only reaches back 7 days.
- Post the list fingerprint in the comments before the draw. It shows the list didn't change after the winners were picked.
- Instagram can't tell any tool who follows you. If your rules require a follow, check it by hand on the winners only.
