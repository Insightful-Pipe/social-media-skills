# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "social-search-optimizer" skill. Find what people search on TikTok and Instagram for my topics, check my last 90 days of videos, and rewrite the captions that are missing a search phrase.

## What you need

- **Nothing connected, if you prefer.** Paste your captions with their views, your profile name and bio, and the search suggestions you see when you type your topics into TikTok and Instagram search.
- **Or the InsightfulPipe MCP,** with your TikTok account (TikTok Pages) and your Instagram business or creator account. The skill then reads your profile, captions and views itself.
- **DataForSEO through InsightfulPipe,** if you want Google US volumes for each phrase and its "tiktok" and "instagram" versions. Without it, the skill ranks phrases on the in-app suggestions alone.
- **Optional:** the on-screen text and first spoken line of your videos. Without them, those spots show as unknown.

## Example requests

**Full search check**
Hey Claude, I just added the "social-search-optimizer" skill. Run a TikTok SEO and Instagram SEO check on my account. My topics are sourdough starter, discard recipes and bread scoring. I pasted the search suggestions below.

**One app only**
Hey Claude, I just added the "social-search-optimizer" skill. Just Instagram: check my Reels captions, my Name field and bio, and alt text, and tell me what to change so I show up in Instagram search.

**Before posting**
Hey Claude, I just added the "social-search-optimizer" skill. Here's my next TikTok script. Pick the search phrase it should target and write the on-screen text, first spoken line, caption and hashtags.

**Content ideas from search**
Hey Claude, I just added the "social-search-optimizer" skill. These are my Creator Search Insights topics. Which ones haven't I covered, and what should the next three videos be?

## What you get

1. **A phrase list** in tiers, with where each phrase was seen and its Google volumes.
2. **A placement map:** where each topic's phrase goes on TikTok and on Instagram.
3. **A profile check** of your display name, Name field, username, bio and alt text, with a rewrite.
4. **A video audit** against your own median views, flagging videos with views and no search phrase.
5. **Rewrites** for the top flagged videos: caption first line, on-screen text, spoken first line and hashtags.
6. **Three next-video briefs** from phrases you haven't covered yet.

## What it can change for you

Nothing. It reads and writes text for you to paste. It never posts, edits or deletes on TikTok or Instagram.

## Tips

- Paste suggestions exactly as the app shows them. The wording people type is the point.
- Run it again a few weeks after the fixes, once the new captions and videos have had time to collect views.
- Google volume shows interest in a topic. Neither app publishes search counts, so treat the tiers as a ranking, not a forecast.
