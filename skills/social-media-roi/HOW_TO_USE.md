# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "social-media-roi" skill. Work out the ROI of our organic social for the last 90 days, per network, and tell me where the team's hours should go.

## What you need

- **Nothing connected is fine.** Paste or upload a GA4 export by source and medium, the same by first user source, your store orders with discount codes, and each network's views and link clicks. The skill works from those.
- **Better: the InsightfulPipe MCP connected to Claude,** with GA4, Shopify or WooCommerce, your social profiles (Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, TikTok, X, YouTube) and your ad accounts (Meta, LinkedIn, TikTok). The skill then pulls everything itself.
- **Your costs:** hours per month on each network (and the fewest that would keep each profile alive), the hourly rate, tools, content production and creator fees.
- **CPMs for X, YouTube or Pinterest,** if you ran ads there: the skill reads Meta, LinkedIn and TikTok CPMs itself, but asks you for these from your ad account's report.
- **What a result is worth:** your gross margin, or the value of one lead or sign-up.
- **Optional:** the discount codes that only run on one network, and your "how did you hear about us" answers.

## Example requests

**The full ROI read**
Hey Claude, I just added the "social-media-roi" skill. Is our organic social worth the hours? Work out the ROI for Instagram, TikTok, Facebook and YouTube for the last 90 days. Our margin is 58%.

**From exports, no connection**
Hey Claude, I just added the "social-media-roi" skill. Here are my GA4 traffic acquisition export, my Shopify orders and our team's hours. Calculate social media ROI per network.

**A lead business**
Hey Claude, I just added the "social-media-roi" skill. We sell software, so there are no orders. A demo request is worth about $400 to us. Show me what LinkedIn and X return for the time we put in.

**Before a budget meeting**
Hey Claude, I just added the "social-media-roi" skill. I have to defend our social team's time next week. Show me the ROI per network with the blind spots stated, so nobody can call the numbers inflated.

## What you get

1. **An ROI range per network:** a floor from tracked sales and code-only sales, and a ceiling that adds assists from GA4's first-user view, each with the model named.
2. **The cost side,** line by line: team time, tools, production and creator fees for the window.
3. **What the reach would have cost** at your own CPMs from your ad accounts, shown beside the ROI and never added to it.
4. **A decision per network:** grow, prove it, fix tracking first, keep for reach or cut back, with the numbers behind each.
5. **Where to put effort:** networks ranked by return per team hour.
6. **A tracking check, the blind spots and a UTM convention** for posts and bio links.

The sample shows it on an invented online candle store: Instagram earns its hours, TikTok can't be judged until its bio link is tagged, Facebook earns its keep in reach, and YouTube's hours are better spent elsewhere.

## What it can change for you

Nothing. It reads and reports. The UTM convention is yours to apply.

## Tips

- Give it 90 days. A month of social sales is too few orders to split by network.
- Give each network its own discount code before the next read. Code sales are the clearest evidence a post sold something tracking missed.
- Run it again 30 days after tagging your bio links, and compare the floor.
