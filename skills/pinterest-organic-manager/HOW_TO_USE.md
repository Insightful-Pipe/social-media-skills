# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "pinterest-organic-manager" skill. Review my Pinterest account, show me my best pins and boards, and plan my next pins from Pinterest Trends.

## What you need

- **The InsightfulPipe MCP connected to Claude, with your Pinterest business account added** (Pinterest Pages). The skill pulls your boards, pins, analytics and Pinterest Trends itself; you don't export anything.
- **A few niche words** that describe what you publish or sell, and the Pinterest interest closest to it (for example `home_decor` or `finance`). Without them, the skill takes words from your board names and pin titles.
- **Optional:** the crawler connected, so it can read a blog post's title, summary and image when you ask for a pin from a post.

## Example requests

**Full review**
Hey Claude, I just added the "pinterest-organic-manager" skill. Review my Pinterest account for the last 30 days. We sell handmade ceramics; use the home_decor interest.

**Top pins and boards**
Hey Claude, I just added the "pinterest-organic-manager" skill. Which of my pins and boards get the most saves and outbound clicks?

**Keywords and seasonal calendar**
Hey Claude, I just added the "pinterest-organic-manager" skill. Find trending Pinterest keywords for small business marketing and tell me when to publish pins for each.

**One pin in depth**
Hey Claude, I just added the "pinterest-organic-manager" skill. Do a deep dive on my newest video pin.

**Pin from a blog post**
Hey Claude, I just added the "pinterest-organic-manager" skill. Turn this blog post into a pin with a keyword-led title and description: https://example.com/blog/my-post. Show me the payload before you publish.

**New board**
Hey Claude, I just added the "pinterest-organic-manager" skill. Draft a board with sections for spring wedding ideas, using trending keywords.

## What you get

1. **A grade out of 100** across five areas: reach and engagement, pin hygiene, board hygiene, freshness and format mix, with the math shown. Areas without enough data say "not graded" instead of guessing.
2. **An account overview** for the last 30 days against the 30 before, or the full 90 days when the account is quiet.
3. **Your top pins and boards,** ranked by saves and outbound clicks.
4. **A format comparison** and the device and source splits Pinterest exposes.
5. **Trending keywords for your niche,** with growth and peak week, and **a seasonal calendar** with a publish-by date for each.
6. **Pin titles, descriptions and alt text** written around those keywords, plus ready payloads for a new pin, board or sections.

## What it can change for you

With your yes on the exact payload, it can:
- publish a new image pin
- create a board and add sections
- update a board's description or hide a test board
- save an existing pin to another board

Pins and boards are public the moment they are created, so it always shows you the board, link and image first. It never deletes anything, and it can't edit the text of a pin that is already live.

## Tips

- Pinterest's analytics only reach the last 90 days, so run it monthly to keep a history.
- Pick the Pinterest interest closest to your niche; Trends lists are per interest.
- Plan seasonal pins from the publish-by dates: pins need time to be picked up before searches peak.
