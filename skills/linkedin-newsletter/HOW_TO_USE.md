# How to use: LinkedIn Newsletter Planner

Hey Claude, I just added the "linkedin-newsletter" skill. Help me start a LinkedIn newsletter for our company Page: pick the topics from what our audience already engages with, and plan the first 12 editions.

## What it needs

Nothing is required: describe your audience and paste a few of your best posts, and it works from that. It gets sharper with:
- **LinkedIn Pages connected through the InsightfulPipe MCP,** so it reads your Page's posts and their impressions, clicks, reactions, comments and reposts, plus your follower counts.
- **Search Console connected,** so edition topics come from the questions people really search for.
- **Your newsletter analytics, pasted,** when you want a review: edition views, new subscribers, email sends and open rate. LinkedIn's API doesn't return newsletter stats, so this part always comes from you.

It asks only what the data can't tell it: who the reader is, what a subscriber should do after reading, how many hours a week you can spend, and when edition 1 goes out.

## Example prompts

**Start a newsletter**
Hey Claude, I just added the "linkedin-newsletter" skill. We want to start a LinkedIn newsletter from our Page. Read our posts and Search Console, then give me a name, a promise, a cadence we can keep with 4 hours a week, and the first 12 editions.

**Newsletter ideas for a founder**
Hey Claude, I just added the "linkedin-newsletter" skill. I'm a founder and want a newsletter on my own profile. Here are my 15 best posts from this year with their numbers. What should it be about, and what should the first editions cover?

**Write the next edition**
Hey Claude, I just added the "linkedin-newsletter" skill. Write edition 5 from our plan, with its promo post. Leave placeholders for any numbers I haven't given you.

**Why isn't it growing?**
Hey Claude, I just added the "linkedin-newsletter" skill. Here are the stats for our first 6 editions from LinkedIn's newsletter analytics. Which editions worked, which themes should we keep, and what should change in the rest of the plan?

## What you get

1. **The lane:** your themes ranked on your own post results and search demand, with the numbers shown.
2. **A name, promise and description,** with two alternatives each.
3. **A cadence** worked out from the hours you have, and the weekday your posts do best.
4. **A dated plan for 12 editions,** each with the question it answers and why it's in the plan.
5. **Edition 1 in full,** plus its promo post, with placeholders for facts only you know.
6. **A subscriber growth plan** built on how LinkedIn invites people to subscribe.
7. **A review of published editions** once you have 4, from the stats you paste.

## What it can do for you

It never publishes an edition: you do that in LinkedIn's editor. Once an edition is live, and with your yes on the exact text, it can publish the promo post from your Page or profile as a link card to the edition. That needs a Read & Write connection, and an owner, admin or operator can switch it off.

## Tips

- Spend the most care on edition 1: it's the one all your followers are invited to subscribe from.
- Draft 3 editions before you publish the first, so a busy week never breaks the schedule.
- After edition 4, paste the stats and let it move the rest of the plan toward what worked.
