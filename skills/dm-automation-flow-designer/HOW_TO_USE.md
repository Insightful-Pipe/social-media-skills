# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "dm-automation-flow-designer" skill. Design an Instagram comment-to-DM flow that sends our lead magnet and collects emails.

## What you need

- **The offer:** what the DM delivers (a checklist, a template, a discount, a booking link) and its URL.
- **Optional, the InsightfulPipe MCP connected to Claude** with your Instagram, Facebook Page or X account, plus GA4 and Klaviyo for measurement. The skill then reads your own posts and comments to pick the trigger. Without a connection, paste a few recent posts with their comment counts and the comments on the best ones.
- **The DM tool you'll run the flow in,** if you have one. The spec loads into any tool approved for Meta's messaging API.
- **If a flow already runs:** its steps and your DM tool's numbers since launch.

## Example requests

**A new Instagram auto DM**
Hey Claude, I just added the "dm-automation-flow-designer" skill. We want an Instagram auto DM for our pricing guide. Pick the post and the word from our comments and write the flow.

**Fix a flow that leaks**
Hey Claude, I just added the "dm-automation-flow-designer" skill. Our comment-to-DM flow gets lots of comments but few emails. Here are the DM tool's numbers. Find where it leaks and redesign it.

**From pasted comments**
Hey Claude, I just added the "dm-automation-flow-designer" skill. I don't have anything connected. Here are the comments on our last three Reels. Which word should trigger the DM, and what should the messages say?

**Facebook and X too**
Hey Claude, I just added the "dm-automation-flow-designer" skill. Turn our Instagram flow into matching flows for our Facebook Page and X, with UTMs so GA4 counts each one.

## What you get

1. **Trigger posts,** ranked against your own account's median, with the ones that fit the offer kept.
2. **The trigger word,** picked from what people already type, with how often each word would fire on comments that never asked.
3. **A step-by-step flow spec** per platform: public reply, opener, a DM-keyword entry, delivery, email ask, one nudge, stop word and hand-off to a person, with every timing checked against Meta's 24-hour window and one-private-reply rule.
4. **A UTM map** for every link, so GA4 counts the clicks, sessions and leads, plus a DM source on every email so Klaviyo counts the leads from each flow.
5. **A preflight** that grades the flow before it goes live, and the funnel with its biggest drop once it runs.

## What it can do for you

With your yes on the exact list and text, it can:
- reply publicly to older comments that asked but never got a DM, inviting them to DM the word, which the flow's DM trigger then answers (Instagram and Facebook)
- send the X DMs to people who replied with the word, with the cost shown first

It never sends Instagram or Facebook DMs itself (your DM tool does that) and never adds people to an email list.

## Tips

- Pick a word people already type. A common word like "yes" fires on praise and DMs people who never asked.
- Put the word in the caption: "Comment TEMPLATE and I'll DM it to you."
- Ask for the email in the DM after delivery, so leads reach your email tool with a source you can count.
- Rerun it two weeks after launch with your DM tool's numbers to find the biggest drop.
